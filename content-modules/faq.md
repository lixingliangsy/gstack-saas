# GotoDeck Growth Console — FAQ

**Q: What is GotoDeck Growth Console?**
A: GotoDeck ties landing-page generation, subscription payments, cross-border compliance scanning, and growth analytics into one console for indie developers and SMB cross-border e-commerce teams.

**Q: Do I need a separate merchant account?**
A: No — GotoDeck processes payments via Waffo (merchant of record). Enterprise plans support BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) and custom payment routing.

**Q: How is my data used?**
A: Only to produce your compliance brief, run your billing, and generate your analytics. We do not train public models on your submissions without explicit consent.

**Q: Which markets do you support?**
A: GotoDeck currently covers EU, UK, US, CA, AU, and JP — with more markets added based on demand.

**Q: Is there a free tier?**
A: Yes — 10 compliance scans per month, basic growth dashboard, and community support. No credit card required.
