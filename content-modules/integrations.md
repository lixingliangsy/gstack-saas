# GStack Growth Console — Integrations

- **Export targets:** JSON download, CSV export, copy-to-clipboard for compliance briefs and analytics reports.
- **API + webhooks:** available on Pro/Enterprise for pipeline embedding — trigger a compliance scan, receive payment events, or push analytics to your data warehouse.
- **Incoming:** works from any tool or workflow that can POST the supported input fields (product description, markets, business model, data handling flags).
- **Payment rail:** Waffo (merchant of record) handles subscriptions; Enterprise supports BYOK payment routing.
- **Planned connectors (not yet shipped):** Shopify, WooCommerce, Stripe Connect, Paddle. Mark each as Planned until live.

> Honesty note: connectors listed as "planned" are NOT yet shipped. Do not advertise them as available. Mark each as Planned until the feature is live and tested.
