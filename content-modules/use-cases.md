# GotoDeck Growth Console — Use cases

| Segment | Pain | How GotoDeck helps |
|---|---|---|
| Independent developers going global | Want to sell to US/EU but lack time or budget to research compliance, set up payments, and build landing pages for each market | GotoDeck scans your product across target markets, generates a compliance brief, handles payment routing, and provides one console for launch |
| SMB cross-border e-commerce teams | Stitch together 3–5 tools (site builder, payment processor, compliance checklist, analytics) with fragmented data | Unified console: build pages, collect subscriptions, monitor compliance posture, and track growth — all in one place |
| Founders validating new markets | Need a fast, low-cost way to assess whether a market is viable before committing resources | Run a free compliance scan to surface obligations and blockers before investing in a full launch |
| Agencies & micro-SaaS consultancies | Need a repeatable, white-labelable workflow to take client products global | GotoDeck provides a structured, exportable pipeline that agencies can embed or resell |
