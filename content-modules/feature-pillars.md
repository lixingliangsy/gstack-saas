# GotoDeck Growth Console — Feature pillars

- **Build — Landing pages, blogs, and SEO pages generated in one click**, optimized for AI search and multi-market distribution.
- **Pay — Subscription billing via Waffo**, covering major EU and North American markets without setting up individual merchant accounts.
- **Comply — Cross-border readiness scan** for GDPR, VAT, consumer protection, app store rules, and payment regulation — mapped to your target markets.
- **Grow — Usage, conversion, funnel, and retention dashboards** that show what's working and where to invest next.

Feature pillars are rendered as capability cards, not a flat 4-item list.
